During my internship in the **AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program from **September 14, 2026** to **December 6, 2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply my academic background in Information Security to a real-world cloud engineering environment.  
Working alongside a 5-member project team, I contributed to designing, implementing, testing, and deploying **PhishGuard AI** — a Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Channel Phishing Triage & SOAR Copilot on AWS Serverless. Through building this system and joining community meetups, I gained practical exposure to cloud architecture, AWS SAM, serverless security concepts, Amazon Bedrock, technical documentation, and teamwork, while still seeing plenty of room to grow in technical depth, communication, and execution pace.

In day-to-day work, I took responsibility for my assigned tasks, followed program guidelines, listened to mentor feedback, and coordinated well with teammates to troubleshoot technical issues and edge cases, though pacing under parallel deadlines and public communication remained areas for improvement.

### Needs Improvement

* **Structured technical presentation & communication:** Practice explaining cloud security designs, trade-offs, and progress updates more concisely and clearly, both in team syncs and public Q&A sessions.
* **Confidence in networking & proactive outreach:** Step out of my comfort zone more during community meetups to network naturally and connect more actively with speakers and peers.
* **Time & workload discipline under tight deadlines:** Manage personal time and sprint pacing more consistently when juggling coding, security testing, and technical writing at the same time.
